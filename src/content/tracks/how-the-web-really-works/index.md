This track follows what happens between typing an address and seeing a page. Five lessons cover the journey from URL to page, HTTP requests and responses, what a web page is made of and how search engines find it, and the cookies and sessions that keep you logged in. You finish by tracing one real page load end to end.

The hands-on parts use your browser's built-in developer tools and `curl`, which ships with macOS, Linux, and Windows 10 and 11. Nothing needs installing, and everything only reads.

Most attacks a defender sees touch the web somewhere: phishing pages, stolen session cookies, scans hitting a web server. This track is what lets you read those logs and alerts. When a question goes beyond the lessons, bring it to the community Discord.
