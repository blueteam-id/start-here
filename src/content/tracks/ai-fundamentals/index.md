This track explains what artificial intelligence and machine learning really are, without hype and without heavy maths. Five lessons cover the key terms, how a model learns from data, the main kinds of learning and where security tools use them, the ways models fail, and how to use AI responsibly at work.

The exercises are done on paper with small examples printed in the lessons. Nothing needs installing, and you never need an account with any AI service.

Security tools increasingly say "AI-powered", and attackers use AI too. Knowing what is really underneath lets you ask the right questions and read the results critically. When a question goes beyond the lessons, bring it to the community Discord.
