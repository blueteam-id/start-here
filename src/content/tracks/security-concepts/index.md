This track is the shared language of security work. Five lessons cover what security protects (the CIA triad), how threats, vulnerabilities, and risk fit together, the basics of hashing and encryption, the ethical and legal lines you must not cross, and a first look at investigating what happened on a computer.

The hands-on parts use your own machine and built-in tools: hashing a file, reading a real vulnerability record, looking up public information about a domain, and building a small timeline from your own computer. Nothing here attacks anything, and Lesson 4 explains exactly why that matters.

This is the bridge from "how computers work" to "how to defend them". When a question goes beyond the lessons, bring it to the community Discord.
